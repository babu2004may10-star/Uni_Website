Featured project · Climate & Society

Designing cities for a warmer future

A five-year study working with local councils to make urban neighbourhoods cooler, greener, and more liveable.

As temperatures rise, the streets and squares of our cities are feeling the heat. The Northbridge Centre for Climate Innovation is working with residents, planners, and public health teams in three neighbourhoods to understand how urban design can reduce heat risk.

The project combines street-level temperature monitoring with community workshops. Early findings show that small interventions — shade, water, trees, and better-connected walking routes — can make a measurable difference to daily life.

Students from environmental science, architecture, and data science are contributing to the research through placements and live projects, putting their learning to work alongside the communities it serves.
